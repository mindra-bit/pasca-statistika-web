1. Pendahuluan

Sistem Tesis Online digunakan untuk mengelola proses tesis mahasiswa S2 Statistika Terapan FMIPA Universitas Padjadjaran secara terdokumentasi, mulai dari pengajuan judul, pembimbingan, penjadwalan SUR/SKR/SAM, revisi, dokumen, publikasi, penilaian, sampai pengiriman laporan lengkap.

Prinsip penggunaan: setiap aktivitas akademik harus memiliki jejak data yang jelas: nama mahasiswa, tahap tesis, tanggal kegiatan, pihak yang terlibat, catatan akademik, tindak lanjut, dan status penyelesaian.

3. Panduan Kaprodi

Akun Kaprodi adalah pusat kendali sistem. Kaprodi memastikan data master benar, tim pembimbing dan penguji lengkap, jadwal tersimpan, dan laporan progress dapat dikirim ke semua pihak terkait.

1. Cek Data MahasiswaPastikan NPM, nama, email mahasiswa, status tesis, Ketua Pembimbing, Anggota Pembimbing, Penguji 1, dan Penguji 2 sudah benar.
2. Tetapkan PengujiPilih mahasiswa, lalu pilih Penguji 1 dan Penguji 2 dari database dosen. Nama dan email penguji otomatis masuk ke data mahasiswa.
3. Atur JadwalPilih tahap SUR/SKR/SAM, isi tanggal, jam, mode, ruang atau tautan, ketua sidang, dan catatan.
4. Monitor ProgressCek log book, revisi, dokumen, publikasi, dan rekap nilai untuk memastikan mahasiswa siap ke tahap berikutnya.
5. Cetak & EmailPilih satu mahasiswa, lalu gunakan preview, download PDF, atau kirim email. Sistem membuat satu file PDF lengkap.
6. Audit DataJika data tidak tampil, gunakan tombol refresh dan cek database Apps Script dengan fungsi diagnostik.

1. Overview

The Online Thesis System manages the Applied Statistics Master's thesis process in a documented workflow, from title submission, supervision, SUR/SKR/SAM scheduling, revisions, documents, publication records, assessment, to complete report delivery.

Operating principle: every academic activity should leave a clear data trail: student name, thesis stage, activity date, people involved, academic notes, follow-up actions, and completion status.

2. User Access Rights

AccountVisible DataMain ActionsLimitations
Head of ProgramAll students, lecturers, schedules, log books, scores, revisions, documents, and publications.Manage master data, examiner assignments, schedules, monitoring, printing, and full report delivery.Score entry is available only when the Head of Program email is listed as an assessor for the student.
SupervisorStudents assigned as supervised students, either under the Main Supervisor or Co-Supervisor role.Review log books, add notes, validate supervision, and enter scores by stage.Cannot modify student master data and cannot view unrelated students.
ExaminerStudents assigned to the lecturer as Examiner 1 or Examiner 2.Review documents, add revisions, and enter rubric-based scores.Cannot modify supervision log books or student master data.
StudentOnly the student's own records.Enter title, log book, documents, publication status, revision follow-up, and view scores.Cannot view other students and cannot enter scores.

3. Head of Program Guide

The Head of Program account controls the system workflow. It ensures that master data, supervisor/examiner teams, schedules, progress records, and reports are complete and accurate.

1. Check Student DataVerify student ID, name, email, thesis status, Main Supervisor, Co-Supervisor, Examiner 1, and Examiner 2.
2. Assign ExaminersSelect a student, then choose Examiner 1 and Examiner 2 from the lecturer database. Names and emails are written to the student data.
3. Set ScheduleSelect SUR/SKR/SAM, then fill in date, time, mode, room or meeting link, session chair, and notes.
4. Monitor ProgressCheck log books, revisions, documents, publications, and score recaps to confirm readiness for the next stage.
5. Print & EmailSelect one student, then use preview, download PDF, or send email. The system generates a complete PDF report.
6. Audit DataIf data is missing, use refresh and check the Apps Script database with the diagnostic function.

4. Supervisor Guide

Supervisors include the Main Supervisor and Co-Supervisor. Both can view their assigned students, review log books, provide academic notes, validate supervision, and enter scores when the assessment stage is open.

Score EntryEnter SUR, SKR, or SAM rubric scores according to the schedule and assessor role.Save scores only after all rubric components are filled.

9. Quick Troubleshooting

ProblemPossible CauseSolution
Student name does not appearStudent data is empty, role access is mismatched, or Code.gs and Index.html are from different packages.Run CEK_DATABASE_YANG_DIBACA(), check Head of Program email, then redeploy the latest version.
Lecturer cannot see studentsLecturer email in the master data differs from the login email.Synchronize emails in Student Data and Lecturer Data.
Student cannot log inEmail/student ID does not match the student master data.Correct the student email or use manual login with email + student ID.
PDF is not sentRecipient email is empty or Apps Script has not been authorized to send email.Complete recipient emails and rerun after authorization.
